Game rule 1 of 3

Reach 50 points

The round continues until you reach 50 points. Earn points by getting other players to return the ball directly to you after you throw it to them.

+1
Every time the player you chose throws the ball straight back to you, you gain one point.

A regular throw to you does not add a point.

Game rule 2 of 3

Do not repeat your choice

You can choose any of the three players whenever you have the ball.

−2
If you choose the same player on two consecutive choices, you lose two points.

You may still choose the same player — the penalty will simply be applied.

Game rule 3 of 3

Special team throw

Sometimes the background will turn light green and the ball will become a star.

SPECIAL THROW
+2
If you pass the star back to the person who gave it to you, every player receives +2 points.
−5
If you pass the star to someone else, you lose 5 points.

This special rule overrides Rule 1 and Rule 2.

On a green/star throw, neither the ordinary +1 return rule nor the repeated-choice −2 rule applies.

Part three

The Decision Game

In this game you will be paired with each of the players (Alex, Robin and Lee). One of you is the Proposer and the other is the Decider. You have been assigned the role of Proposer.

How the game works

  1. In each round, you receive 10 points = $1.00.
  2. You decide how to split the 10 points between yourself and the other player (Decider). You can offer any amount from 0 to 10 points in whole units. For example, you might offer 5 and keep 5, offer 2 and keep 8, or choose any other division.
  3. The Decider sees your offer and chooses whether to accept or reject it. If the Decider accepts, the points are divided as you proposed. If the Decider rejects, neither of you receives anything in that round.
  4. You make only one offer to each player and cannot change it after submitting. There is no negotiation or communication between you.

Important points

  • Your decisions are anonymous. The Decider will not learn who you are, and you will not learn who they are; you will only receive their decision at the end.
  • There are no right or wrong answers. Please decide according to what you actually want to do in each case.
  • Payment: At the end of the study, one of the outcomes from your three offers will be selected at random and used for your additional bonus payment.
